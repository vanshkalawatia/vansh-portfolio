# 📝 Update My Portfolio

You have two easy ways to update your portfolio content:

---

## ⚡ Method 1: Edit `PORTFOLIO_CONFIG.ts` Directly (Instant Live Hot-Reload)

All content across your entire website is now controlled by one central file located at the root of your project:

👉 **[`PORTFOLIO_CONFIG.ts`](./PORTFOLIO_CONFIG.ts)**

When your local dev server is running (`npm.cmd run dev`), any change you save in `PORTFOLIO_CONFIG.ts` **instantly updates in your browser at `http://localhost:5000`** without restarting anything!

### Sections you can change in `PORTFOLIO_CONFIG.ts`:
- **`personalInfo`**: Your name, headline, bio, email, GitHub, LinkedIn, LeetCode, X/Twitter, Instagram, and resume URL.
- **`heroPhrases`**: The rotating typewriter tagline below your name.
- **`educationData`**: Degrees, universities, and time periods.
- **`certificationsData`**: NPTEL certifications, titles, issuers, and years.
- **`experiencesData`**: Student journey, milestones, and focus areas.
- **`projectsData`**: Your projects, descriptions, tech stacks, GitHub links, and live preview URLs.
- **`skillsData`**: Technical categories (Python & Backend, Security, Databases, DSA, DevOps, Web).
- **`blogsData`**: Blog posts, article titles, summaries, dates, tags, and links.
- **`interestsData`**: Hobbies, fun facts, emojis, and descriptions.
